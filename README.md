# 📊 Mental Health Survey - Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project performs an end-to-end Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a mental health survey dataset from the tech industry. The goal is to understand how workplace factors, demographics, and organizational support impact mental health and treatment-seeking behavior.

---

## 🧹 Data Cleaning & Preprocessing
- Standardized inconsistent gender values into **Male, Female, Other**
- Converted Age column to numeric and handled invalid entries
- Removed unrealistic values (e.g., negative ages, extreme outliers)
- Performed data validation to ensure quality analysis

---

## 📊 Exploratory Data Analysis
Key analyses performed:
- Age and Gender distribution
- Country-wise participation
- Mental health treatment patterns
- Workplace factors (remote work, company size, benefits)
- Awareness and support systems (wellness programs, anonymity, leave policies)

---

## 🔍 Key Insights
- Majority of respondents are aged **25–35**, indicating a young workforce
- Individuals with a **family history of mental illness** are more likely to seek treatment
- Mental health issues significantly impact **work productivity**
- Many organizations **lack proper mental health support systems**
- Awareness about seeking help is present but inconsistent

---

## 📈 Relationship Analysis
- Strong correlation between **family history and treatment**
- Work interference is a key indicator of mental health challenges
- Organizational support plays a critical role in treatment-seeking behavior

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations
- Implement structured mental health support programs
- Improve awareness and communication about available help
- Encourage open conversations to reduce stigma
- Provide confidential and accessible care options

---

## ⚠️ Limitations
- Survey-based dataset (possible response bias)
- Majority data from tech industry
- Self-reported responses may not be fully accurate
